Narrow AI and General AI

Most AI systems in use today are narrow AI. A narrow AI system is built to do one specific task or a small set of related tasks well. A spam filter, a translation app, and a face-unlock feature are all narrow AI. Each one performs its task within limits, and it cannot simply switch to a different task on its own.

General AI, sometimes called artificial general intelligence, would be a system able to learn and perform a wide range of intellectual tasks, the way a person can. It could move between subjects, transfer what it learned in one area to another, and handle problems it was never specifically built for. Such a system does not exist yet, and experts disagree about when, or whether, it will be built.

The difference matters because the two are often confused in news and conversation. A system that writes fluent text or answers many questions can look general, but it may still be narrow in how it works, trained for particular kinds of patterns and tasks.

When you read about AI, check which kind is being described. A claim about a narrow tool, such as a photo classifier, is very different from a claim about a system that can do anything a person can do.
